Training Load Risk Checker

A quick look at your acute-to-chronic workload ratio to spot whether your recent training load is increasing too quickly.

ACWR = this week's load ÷ your rolling 4-week average. In practice, a ratio around 0.85–1.25 is usually the most sustainable zone, while values above about 1.5 are more likely to reflect a rapid spike in workload. This is a simplified educational estimate, not medical advice.
