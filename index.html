Training Load Injury Risk Calculator

Based on the Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio (ACWR) — the same metric sports scientists use to flag injury risk before it happens.

ACWR = this week's training load ÷ average load over the last 4 weeks. A ratio between 0.8–1.3 is generally considered a safe training zone. Above 1.5 is associated with meaningfully elevated injury risk in sports science literature. This is a simplified educational estimate, not medical advice.
